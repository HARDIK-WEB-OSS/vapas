VAPAS privacy notice (version 2026-11-v1)

What we collect
- Your email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash).
- The bank statements you upload, and the transactions we read from them. Statements can contain names, UPI IDs and account details of other people you paid or who paid you.
- Technical records needed for security: sign-in times and a hashed form of your IP address.

Why we use it
- Only to find failed or late-reversed transactions, duplicate debits and similar items, to calculate any compensation under RBI rules, and to help you draft complaints that you review and send yourself.
- We do not sell your data, show you ads, or use your statements for any other purpose.

How we protect it
- The original statement file is deleted within 1 hour of upload, after it is read.
- Transaction details are encrypted with a key unique to you.
- Text from your statements is never sent to an outside AI service.
- We never connect to your bank, never move money and never file anything on your behalf.

How long we keep it
- Transactions are deleted 13 months after their date. Findings and your cases are kept until you delete them or your account.

Your rights
- Download all your data at any time from Settings.
- Delete your account and all your data at any time from Settings.
- Withdraw this consent at any time from Settings. We then stop processing your statements.
- If you are not satisfied with how we handle a complaint about your data, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.

VAPAS is not legal advice.
